How To Use This Site

A quick walkthrough of taking a test from start to finish.

  1. 1

    Select Practice Tests or Mock Tests from the navigation menu.

  2. 2

    Select a test card and click Start Test (or Start Mock Test).

  3. 3

    Read the question carefully. Only one question is shown at a time.

  4. 4

    Select one answer option (A, B, C or D).

  5. 5

    Click Submit Answer.

  6. 6

    The website immediately shows whether your answer is correct or incorrect.

  7. 7

    Read the correct answer and the explanation provided beneath it.

  8. 8

    Click Next Question to continue.

  9. 9

    Continue answering until you reach the final question.

  10. 10

    View your final score, then use Review Answers to go back over every question, or Retake Test to try again.

Test Formats

25

Questions per Practice Test — 24 topic-wise tests in total.

50

Questions per Mock Test — 16 full-length tests in total.

A Few Notes

Once you submit an answer, the four options are locked — you cannot change your answer for that question. Your progress bar and question counter update automatically as you move through the test.

Your most recent test scores can be stored in your browser (using localStorage) so you can track your progress. This data stays on your device only; you can clear it at any time using the Reset Progress link in the footer.
