About HAM RADIO TESTS

HAM RADIO TESTS is a free, self-paced practice platform designed to help students prepare for their Amateur Radio (HAM Radio) examination. It offers topic-wise practice tests and full-length mock tests covering Basic Electronics, Radio Theory, Radio Equipment, Basic Operating Procedures, Rules & Regulations, and Safety.

What This Site Offers

24 practice tests (25 questions each) let you focus on one topic at a time, with an explanation provided after every question so you understand not just the correct answer but the reasoning behind it. 16 mock tests (50 questions each) simulate a full-length exam by drawing questions from across the entire syllabus.

How It Works

The entire site runs in your browser. Questions are loaded from CSV files, and no personal data, login, or account is required to use it. Your test scores, if you choose to keep them, are stored only in your own browser using localStorage — they are never sent anywhere.

Please note: HAM RADIO TESTS is an independent study and practice resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by any government, telecommunications authority, or official examination body, and completing tests on this site does not constitute official certification or guarantee examination results. Always refer to your national licensing authority for official syllabus material and examination requirements.
